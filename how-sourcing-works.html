Process

How Sourcing Works

A straightforward desk between your procurement team and qualified China suppliers — specs in, comparable quote out, then packing and corridor freight.

Send an RFQ

Company, country & project site, category, item list/specs, quantity, needed-by date, Incoterm preference and whether certificates are required. Fleet RFQs add make/model and OEM-or-alternative preference.

We clarify & source

We check specs against supplier coverage, ask for missing details (grades, drawings, standards), and shortlist options that match your corridor and compliance notes.

Preliminary quote (24–48h on standard items)

Line items, unit and extended pricing, Incoterm, lead time and certificate status. Complex fleet or custom lines may take longer — we say so up front.

Confirm & produce

You approve the quote. We place orders, manage packing and export paperwork, and keep you updated on production milestones.

Ship into your corridor

Freight into Sydney, Auckland, Port Moresby or another agreed Oceania port. You handle last-mile unless a landed Incoterm was quoted.

What we are not. Not a retail store, not a local AOG breakdown service, and not an engineering sign-off authority. Fitment and site application remain the buyer’s responsibility.

Best suited for

Repeat consumables, planned outages, project mobilisation packs and non-emergency fleet parts where China lead times fit your schedule.

Certificates

When you mark certificates required, we only quote lines where suppliers can provide documentable evidence. Temporary power & lighting is always certification-gated.
