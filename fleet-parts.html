Fleet & Equipment Parts

We do not publish a parts catalogue. Heavy equipment and fleet parts are quoted case-by-case against make/model, part numbers and your OEM-or-alternative preference.

RFQ desk — not a parts warehouse. Send equipment details and we source options through our China supplier network. Fitment and application remain the buyer’s responsibility; we support documentation and packing for corridor delivery.

What to include

Make and model, OEM part numbers or drawings, quantity, fleet vs site use, and whether OEM-only or quality alternatives are acceptable. Photos of nameplates help.

What you get back

Options with lead time, Incoterm, packing notes and any available certificates. Complex or obsolete lines may take longer than the 24–48h consumables window.

Typical scope

Wear parts, filters, ground-engaging tools, hydraulic components, electrics and other service parts for mining and construction fleets — subject to supplier coverage.

Not suitable for

Emergency breakdown same-day local supply, retail automotive, or items requiring on-site engineering sign-off we cannot support. Use local dealers for critical-path AOG needs.
