Freight into Oceania

Delivery Corridors

We consolidate and ship into three primary hubs — Sydney, Auckland and Port Moresby — with other Oceania ports available on request.

Sydney

Primary AU corridor for mines and construction sites across Australia. Sea and air options quoted per shipment.

Auckland

Primary NZ corridor for North Island and onward domestic distribution. Incoterms and packing set on the quote.

Port Moresby

Primary PNG corridor for mining and project sites. Remote inland delivery timing depends on local onward freight.

Other Oceania ports on request. Fiji, Solomon Islands, New Caledonia, Vanuatu and other Pacific gateways can be quoted when you state destination, volume and preferred Incoterm on the RFQ.

What we arrange

Supplier packing, consolidation where useful, export documentation support, and freight options into the corridor hub named on your quote.

What you control

Incoterm preference (e.g. FOB, CIF, DAP), destination detail, and whether you handle last-mile from the hub with your own logistics partner.

Lead times

Production plus ocean or air transit. Standard consumables: preliminary quote in 24–48 hours; transit depends on mode and corridor.

Duties & compliance

Import duties, GST/VAT and local compliance remain the buyer’s responsibility unless we explicitly quote a landed Incoterm covering them.
